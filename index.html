People & Craft

The story behind the pixels.

✦ Senior UI/UX Designer

Creative & calligraphy expert turned senior UI/UX designer. I've spent the last 3+ years designing for software houses and companies who need more than "clean and modern" — they need interfaces with a point of view.

My process leans on structure — user flows, sitemaps, wireframes — but the details are where the calligraphy background shows: type rhythm, spacing, the little curves that make an interface feel considered instead of assembled.

Day to day that means everything from mobile app UI and dashboards to landing pages, e‑commerce and full brand identities, taken from a blank canvas to a clickable, developer‑ready prototype.
